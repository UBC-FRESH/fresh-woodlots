# Project brief

## One sentence

Give every woodlot licence in British Columbia a free, public, browser-based
strategic and tactical planning workspace built from public data, so a small
woodlot holder can ask landscape and block-level "what if" questions without
buying a model, a server, or a consultant.

## Users

- Woodlots BC staff and member woodlot licensees.
- Woodlot holders, families, and small operators.
- First Nation partners and local governments.
- Students and researchers who need a transparent public benchmark.

## What we are building

1. A catalog of every active woodlot licence in BC from the public forest
   tenure layer.
2. One WS4 strategic instance per woodlot with public AAC.
3. One first-period FHOPS operational pilot per built woodlot.
4. A portal with a province map, per-woodlot pages, saved scenarios, sources,
   terms, and privacy.

## Public data

Forest Tenure Managed Licence, issued woodlot AAC report (`A04`), VRI 2025,
Provincial Proxy THLB, VDYP7 input tables, TIPSY tables, and TSA/site
productivity. No private licensee data.

## Caveats

The Proxy THLB is strategic, not statutory. TIPSY species surrogates are
recorded. VDYP still runs under a time-boxed Wine exception. FHOPS plans are
first-period pilots. This is not a statutory timber supply analysis.
